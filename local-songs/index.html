Using and editing these scores

Keep each SONATA source beside its matching score data file. The local guide includes commands to execute the scores and rebuild the recordings.

The supplied MIDI controls the notes, timing, instruments, and percussion. These recordings use FluidR3 GM sounds and include a short release tail. They reproduce your downloaded arrangements.

Playback runs locally. See README.md and MEDIA_NOTICE.md for source provenance and licensing.
