# Third-party song material

The song conversions in this directory come from user-supplied MIDI arrangements of **Megalovania**, **Bad Apple**, and **A Cruel Angel's Thesis**. The original download filenames were `Megalovania.mid`, `badapple!!midifull.mid`, and `Evangelion - Cruel Angel's Thesis.mid`. Arrangement authors and redistribution licenses were not supplied with those files. The verification reports preserve their original file hashes.

The repository's MIT license covers the original SONATA software and original demonstration music. It does not grant rights to these third-party compositions or arrangements, including their converted `.son` catalogs, `.inputs.json` schedules, MIDI files, and synthesized recordings. Rights in that material remain with the respective rights holders. No ownership or MIT licensing of the songs is asserted.

FluidR3 GM is a separate MIT-licensed SoundFont by Frank Wen. Its license and package provenance are retained under `assets/`; the font itself is downloaded separately. The recordings use synthesized instruments, not samples from commercial song recordings.
